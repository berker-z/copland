wired box prototype

The box shows what the daemon picked up from your boards. Items wait on the wire into todo, get picked up at doing, where a run holds them while current flows in, and leave doing by one of two spans: the short one to blocked, half a span on, where it waits for you, or the long one that sags deep under blocked's arms and rises to done, then runs off the edge. All four poles stand on one ground line, each with its tickets underneath; lamps and beads take the stage colours.
