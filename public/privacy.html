Privacy Policy

copland · copland.berkerz.dev · last updated 1 October 2026

Copland is a personal dashboard and project tracker. This instance, at copland.berkerz.dev, is run by berker-z for themselves and people they invite. It is not a commercial service, shows no ads, and has no analytics. Copland is also open source; copies that other people host are run by them, and this policy covers only this instance.

What it stores

  • Your Google account email, name and profile picture, from signing in.
  • What you create: tasks, boards, comments, labels, notes, and your settings (theme, weather location, the coins you follow).
  • API keys you choose to save for panes that need them (CoinGecko, OpenAI). They are encrypted before they are stored and are never sent back to your browser.
  • If you connect Google Calendar: a refresh token for each Google account you connect, encrypted before it is stored, and the list of that account's calendars with which ones you chose to show. Calendar events themselves are not stored; they are read from Google when the dashboard asks for them.
  • If you add a calendar by ICS link: the link, encrypted, since those links are usually secret.
  • If you connect an AI assistant or make an API token: a record of the token (only a hash of its secret), its name, and when it was last used.
  • A history of changes on boards (who changed what, and when), shown to the board's members.

Who can see it

Your settings, notes, saved keys and calendars are visible only to you. Your inbox board is private. Anything you put on a board you share (tasks, comments, your name on assignments) is visible to that board's members. The operator of this instance can technically read the database, but does not look at people's data except to fix a problem you have asked them to fix.

Where it is stored

On Cloudflare, which hosts the app (Cloudflare Workers) and its database (Cloudflare D1). Sign-in is a cookie holding a random session token; the app sets no other cookies. Your browser also keeps your theme and the notepad's open note in local storage, only to draw the page faster.

How Google data is used

Your Google sign-in is used only to know who you are. Calendar access, if you grant it, is used only to show your calendars in the dashboard and to create, edit and delete events when you ask it to. Google data is never sold, never shared with anyone else, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models or for anything beyond the features you see in the app. If you connect an AI assistant, it can read your calendar events through that connection only because you created it, and only while you keep it.

Copland's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

  • Google, for sign-in and (if you connect it) Calendar.
  • Binance and Open-Meteo, called directly by your browser for crypto prices, weather and the city search. They receive an ordinary web request and nothing about your account.
  • CoinGecko and OpenAI, called by the server with your own saved key, only if you saved one. OpenAI receives the text you type into the verse pane.
  • Any ICS link you add, which the server fetches to read the calendar.

Retention and removal

Disconnecting a Google account in settings deletes its stored token and calendar list, and tells Google to revoke the access. You can also revoke it from Google's side at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing a saved key, a feed or a token deletes it. To have your account and everything in it deleted, write to the address below.
